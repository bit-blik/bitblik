# Prima banka — SELL Lightning for cash at an ATM

For the person who wants **cash from a Prima banka ATM** in exchange for
Lightning. You do **not** need a Prima banka account — you only type a code at
the ATM. (In BitBlik you are the **maker/seller**.)

> ⚠️ See the ToS/legal note in [README](README.md#-important--dôležité-upozornenie).
>
> 📝 The ATM steps below still need a pass against a real Prima banka ATM —
> correct them (and drop this note) once someone has walked through it.

## EN

### You need
- BitBlik SK app (Slovakia market selected).
- A Lightning wallet with sats to lock (+ small fee).
- To be **near a Prima banka ATM** and online when the buyer is ready.

### Steps
1. **BitBlik → Slovensko** 🇸🇰 (pick this market on first launch; later in
   **Settings → Country / Payment System**). Create a **sell offer**, choose
   **Prima banka** as the bank and the **€ amount** (10/20/50/100/200), then
   confirm. `[APP: maker amount form]`
2. **Fund the hold invoice** (locks your sats). Wait for a taker.
   `[APP: "1. Create Offer > 2. Wait for Taker > 3. Use code"]`
3. On a match, BitBlik shows a **6-digit code**, valid **30 min**.
   `[APP: offer details, 6-digit code + copy]`
4. **At a Prima banka ATM** (no card inserted):
   1. On the start screen, choose the **cardless withdrawal** option.
   2. **Enter the code**, press confirm.
   3. The ATM shows the **amount from the buyer's app** → confirm (cannot edit).
   4. The ATM dispenses the cash.
5. Back in BitBlik press **"Confirm successful payment"** → sats released. Done.

### Tips
- The code lives **30 min** — the roomiest of the four SK banks, so you can
  still be walking to the ATM when it arrives.
- **€200** max per withdrawal, so a Prima banka offer never goes above that.
- Prima banka ATMs only; no PIN at the ATM.
- If it fails, use **"Invalid code"** so the buyer isn't charged.

Bank reference (Peňaženka + cardless withdrawal): https://www.primabanka.sk/penazenka

## SK

### Čo potrebuješ
- Appku BitBlik SK (zvolený trh **Slovensko**).
- Lightning peňaženku so sats na uzamknutie (+ malý poplatok).
- Byť **pri bankomate Prima banky** a online, keď je kupujúci pripravený.

### Postup
1. **BitBlik → Slovensko** 🇸🇰 (pri prvom spustení vyber tento trh; neskôr v
   **Settings → Country / Payment System**). Vytvor **ponuku na predaj**, ako
   banku zvoľ **Prima banka**, zadaj **sumu v €** (10/20/50/100/200) a potvrď.
   `[APP: formulár sumy]`
2. **Zaplať hold faktúru** (uzamkne sats). Počkaj na protistranu.
   `[APP: „1. Vytvor ponuku > 2. Čakaj > 3. Použi kód"]`
3. Pri spárovaní BitBlik ukáže **6-miestny kód**, platný **30 min**.
   `[APP: detail ponuky, kód + kopírovať]`
4. **Pri bankomate Prima banky** (nevkladáš kartu):
   1. Na úvodnej obrazovke zvoľ **výber bez karty**.
   2. **Zadaj kód**, potvrď.
   3. Bankomat ukáže **sumu z appky kupujúceho** → potvrď (nedá sa meniť).
   4. Bankomat vydá hotovosť.
5. V BitBliku stlač **„Potvrdiť úspešnú platbu"** → sats sa uvoľnia. Hotovo.

### Tipy
- Kód platí **30 min** — najdlhšie zo štyroch slovenských bánk, takže môžeš byť
  ešte na ceste k bankomatu, keď príde.
- Max **200 €** na výber, takže ponuka pre Prima banku nikdy nejde vyššie.
- Len bankomaty Prima banky; pri bankomate netreba PIN.
- Ak to zlyhá, použi **„Neplatný kód"**, aby kupujúcemu nič nestrhlo.
